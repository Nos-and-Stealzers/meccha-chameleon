How to play

Hiding

WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Shift sneak
F paint mode · 1-5 poses · Z/X turn · C clone decoy · Q taunt (points, but they hear it) · Enter skip prep

Use the eyedropper on the surface you're hiding against, block in the colours, then pose. Flat snaps your back to the nearest wall. Hunters judge you on what they actually see: contrast with what's behind you, your shadow and movement all count. Moving while watched is a giveaway.

Seeking

WASD move · Mouse look · Click bonk · Space jump

Look for colour blobs that don't belong, shapes repeated in the wrong spot, odd bumps on walls and floors. Hiders are forced to whistle every so often, so listen.
